# Healthcare for international students in Hungary

Status: researched 2026-11-21 from university and official pages. Rules differ by university and scholarship type, so always point the student to their international office.

## Emergency numbers
- 112: general emergency number (ambulance, fire, police). Free, works from any phone, 24/7.
- 104: ambulance. 105: fire. 107: police.
- Source: https://studyinhungary.hu/living-in-hungary/menu/emergency-numbers.html ; https://travel.gc.ca/assistance/embassies-consulates/hungary

## TAJ card (social security / state health insurance card)
- TAJ = Társadalombiztosítási Azonosító Jel, the number and card for Hungary's public health insurance.
- Issued by NEAK (Nemzeti Egészségbiztosítási Alapkezelő, National Health Insurance Fund), NOT by NAV (the tax office). Some universities say you submit at the government office (kormányhivatal) instead. The exact office depends on the university, so the student should follow the international office's instructions.
- Scholarship holders (Stipendium Hungaricum, Christian Young People, Diaspora) are entitled to a TAJ card and free public healthcare. The university's international/scholarship coordinator usually helps with the application.
- The TAJ application can usually only start after the student has the residence permit. First you get the TAJ number; the card arrives a few weeks later.
- Until the TAJ card arrives, scholarship holders have supplementary private insurance paid by the scholarship programme. Carry the letter of award, student status certificate and passport with residence permit.
- Self-funded (fee-paying) students from outside the EEA are NOT entitled to free public healthcare. They need private insurance (often arranged by the university) or a paid NEAK agreement (monthly contribution based on the minimum wage; check the current amount).
- Use the TAJ card together with a valid passport.
- Sources: https://semmelweis.hu/registrar/information/taj-card/ ; https://u-szeged.hu/english/health-issues/taj-step-by-step-guide ; https://www.elte.hu/en/content/students-with-stipendium-hungaricum-or-scholarship-programme-for-christian-young-people.t.1081 ; https://inf.elte.hu/en/taj

## Doctors (GP / háziorvos), specialists, pharmacies
- See your GP (háziorvos, family doctor) first. Specialists usually need a GP referral (beutaló), except in emergencies.
- Medicine: take the prescription (paper or e-prescription) to a pharmacy (gyógyszertár / patika).
- Source: https://biztositas.unideb.hu/medical-treatments

## University of Debrecen (UniDeb) students
- UniDeb offers a compulsory combined health and travel insurance (STUDIUM / Generali), with the fee included in tuition for students who need it. Students without an EHIC must have it.
- The GP for insured international students is at Egyetem tér 1, in the building behind the Coordinating Center for International Education. Opening hours and the GP's name differ between UniDeb pages, so check the current details on the insurance site before going.
- Specialist referrals go through the University's Clinical Center.
- Sick notes are only given in person at the GP office.
- Outside office hours: the Emergency Clinic (Emergency and Gastroenterology Building), or call 104 for an ambulance.
- Medicines for conditions you had before arriving are not covered by the insurance.
- Sources: https://biztositas.unideb.hu/medical-treatments ; https://insurance.unideb.hu/faq ; https://biztositas.unideb.hu/general-information
