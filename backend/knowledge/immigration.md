# Immigration and official paperwork (non-EEA students)

Status: researched 2026-11-21. The immigration office is OIF (Országos Idegenrendészeti Főigazgatóság, National Directorate-General for Aliens Policing). Rules change often: always tell the student to confirm on oif.gov.hu or with their international office.

## Renewing (extending) a residence permit for study
- Apply ONLY online through the Enter Hungary portal (enterhungary.gov.hu), then attend the client service appointment if asked.
- Timing: OIF says submit at least 30 days before the permit expires; for permits issued from 2024 the window is no earlier than 45 days and no later than 30 days before expiry. Many universities advise starting around 45 days before. Check the window for your own permit.
- Do not let it expire: once the permit has expired you generally cannot start an extension from inside Hungary.
- Typical documents: valid passport, student status certificate from the university, accommodation proof, proof of financial means, plus the fee. The exact list is shown in Enter Hungary and on OIF's factsheet.
- Student mobility permits cannot be extended.
- Sources: https://oif.gov.hu/factsheets/extend-your-permit-student ; https://oif.gov.hu/students ; https://www.elte.hu/en/content/extension-of-residence-permit.t.2799?m=649

## Address registration (lakcímkártya vs accommodation certificate)
- Most non-EEA students with a student residence permit do NOT get a lakcímkártya (address card). They register their accommodation with the immigration office and receive an accommodation certificate (often with a QR code). Keep it with you.
- Report a change of accommodation to the immigration office (online via Enter Hungary).
- The lakcímkártya is mainly for Hungarian/EEA citizens and for third-country nationals with permanent residence.
- Sources: https://oif.gov.hu/factsheets/reporting-obligations ; https://oif.gov.hu/news/change-as-of-1-may-2025

## Tax ID (adóazonosító jel) — needed for jobs, internships, some scholarships
- Issued by NAV (Nemzeti Adó- és Vámhivatal, National Tax and Customs Administration) using form T34.
- First application: on paper, in person at a NAV customer service desk (or by post). Free of charge. Apply before you receive any income.
- Students usually then enter the tax number in Neptun.
- Sources: https://nav.gov.hu/en/taxation/taxpayer_registration/general-information-for-foreign-citizens-new ; https://nav.gov.hu/en/main-tiles/how-to-apply-for-a-tax-card
