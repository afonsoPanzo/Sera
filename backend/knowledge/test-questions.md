## How do I get a TAJ card? I have a Stipendium Hungaricum scholarship.
topic: healthcare
language: en
Must say: TAJ is issued by NEAK (not NAV); scholarship holders are entitled; usually after the residence permit; the international/scholarship coordinator helps; private insurance covers until the card arrives.
Must NOT say: apply at NAV.
Source: healthcare.md

## I'm a self-funded student from Nigeria. Is healthcare free for me in Hungary?
topic: healthcare
language: en
Must say: no, self-funded non-EEA students are not entitled to free public healthcare; private insurance (UniDeb: compulsory STUDIUM insurance) or a paid NEAK agreement.
Source: healthcare.md

## I'm sick at night in Debrecen and the GP is closed. What do I do?
topic: healthcare
language: en
Must say: emergency → 112 (or 104 ambulance); Emergency Clinic at the Emergency and Gastroenterology Building outside GP hours.
Source: healthcare.md

## How do I register for an exam in Neptun at the University of Debrecen?
topic: university
language: en
Must say: Exams menu → exam registration; deadline 12:00 noon the day before; no registration = cannot sit the exam; unpaid items block registration.
Must NOT say: invented deregistration windows (e.g. "24-48 hours").
Source: university.md

## How do I get my student card?
topic: university
language: en
Must say: NEK datasheet at a government office (kormányablak) with passport and residence permit; then Neptun student card request with the NEK ID; foreign permanent address; temporary card possible.
Must NOT say: fingerprints.
Source: university.md

## When should I renew my student residence permit?
topic: immigration
language: en
Must say: online via Enter Hungary only; at least 30 days before expiry, recent permits 45 to 30 days before; don't let it expire; confirm on oif.gov.hu.
Source: immigration.md

## Do I need a lakcímkártya as a non-EU student?
topic: immigration
language: en
Must say: most non-EEA students with a student permit don't get a lakcímkártya; they register accommodation with the immigration office and get an accommodation certificate; report changes via Enter Hungary.
Source: immigration.md
