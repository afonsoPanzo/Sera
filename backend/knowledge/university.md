# University administration (Neptun, exams, student card)

Status: researched 2026-11-21. Menu names in Neptun differ slightly between universities. Details below are for the University of Debrecen (UniDeb) unless stated otherwise.

## Exam registration in Neptun (UniDeb)
- You must register in Neptun to sit an exam. Without registration you are not allowed to take it.
- Path: Exams menu → exam registration ("Take exam"). The same button registers ("Take") and deregisters ("Drop").
- Deadline at UniDeb: until 12:00 noon the day before the exam.
- Students with unpaid items in Neptun cannot register, so pay outstanding fees first (top up Neptun at least 3 working days before a payment is due).
- Exam dates can have limited seats, so register early.
- Final exams can use a different menu (Administration → Final exams). Help: neptun@unideb.hu
- Sources: https://neptun.unideb.hu/en/students/exam-registration ; https://mek.unideb.hu/en/useful-information-about-exam-period-and-end-study-period

## Student card (diákigazolvány)
- Step 1: get a NEK datasheet (NEK adatlap) at any government office (kormányablak). They take your photo and record your data; bring passport and residence permit. No fingerprints are mentioned in any guide. The NEK ID is printed at the top right of the sheet.
- Step 2: in Neptun, go to Administration → Student card request → add a new request, type "first application", and enter the NEK ID in capital letters without hyphens.
- International students must use their foreign permanent address in the request, and the data must match Neptun exactly, or the request is rejected.
- The first card is free. A temporary student card (valid 60 days) can usually be requested at the student affairs office while you wait.
- Sources: https://hak.unideb.hu/student-card ; https://u-szeged.hu/student-card ; https://semmelweis.hu/phd/en/practical-info/student-card/
